# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, no harassment of any kind, and keep
disagreements technical. The full text at the link above is the binding version.

One project-specific addition, because of what this software does: **discussions
and contributions that frame Markwright as a tool for surveilling individuals,
profiling authors, or "catching" people will be closed.** The product's
responsible-use position ([docs/limitations.md](docs/limitations.md)) is part of
the project, not marketing copy.

Reports: open a private security advisory on GitHub or contact the maintainers
listed in the repository. Maintainers will respond within 5 business days and
may warn, moderate, or ban for violations.
